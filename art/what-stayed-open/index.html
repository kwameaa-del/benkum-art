What Stayed Open

Some choices are made by what we keep returning to.

There was another plan. The closed book belongs to it. She still opens it, just less often than the other one.

The other book fills with recipes, then margin notes, then whole pages in her own shorthand.

On September 23, 2026, Rindwine Amadhila, 30, of Oshakati, was profiled for spending part of her university study loan on baking courses. She now runs Kalindi Bakery alongside her studies. “Each order is special to me,” she says, “because behind every cake is a customer trusting me to be part of an important moment in their life.”

Both books stay on the shelf. Her hand rests on the open one, in no hurry to be anywhere else.
